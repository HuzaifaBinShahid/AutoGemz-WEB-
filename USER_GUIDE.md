# AutoGemZ User Guide

Follow these simple steps to start bidding and winning your dream car today.

## 1. ACCOUNT ACCESS

Start your journey by signing up or logging into your AutoGemZ account to unlock full access to our premium auction marketplace. Your profile ensures all your bids and vehicles are securely tracked.

## 2. AUCTION PARTICIPATION

Browse our curated list of live auctions. Click **"Join Live"** to send a participation request to our admin team. Once approved, you'll gain full bidding access to that specific auction.

## 3. REAL-TIME BIDDING

Once approved, enter the live bidding arena. Place your bids strategically in real-time and track the countdown to secure your vehicle. Click **"SET BID"** to confirm your offer.

## 4. DASHBOARD MANAGEMENT

Track all your activity in one place. Your **"My Auction Cars"** dashboard shows your active bids, won vehicles, lost auctions, and historical data for complete transparency.
